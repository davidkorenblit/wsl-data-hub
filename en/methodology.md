      <div class="space-y-3">
        
        <!-- Question 1 -->
        <div class="p-4 rounded border border-neutral-200 dark:border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-50 dark:bg-[#18181b] space-y-1.5">
          <div class="flex items-center gap-2">
            <span class="px-1.5 py-0.5 border border-neutral-300 dark:border-neutral-700 bg-white dark:bg-neutral-900 text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300 text-[11px] font-mono font-bold rounded">Q1</span>
            <h3 class="font-bold text-neutral-900 dark:text-white text-base">Squad Baseline & Vulnerability Mapping</h3>
          </div>
          <p class="text-sm text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
            "How did the club perform across the three phases of play (goalkeeping & defense, midfield control, and attacking output), and what were the critical deficiencies requiring summer intervention?"
          </p>
          <div class="text-xs text-neutral-500 dark:text-neutral-400 pt-1 leading-relaxed">
            <strong>Key Metrics:</strong> Post-Shot xG minus Goals Allowed (PSxG+/-), field tilt, progressive passes, shot volumes, and box touches. This step formalizes the club's precise summer shopping list.
          </div>
        </div>

        <!-- Question 2 -->
        <div class="p-4 rounded border border-neutral-200 dark:border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-50 dark:bg-[#18181b] space-y-1.5">
          <div class="flex items-center gap-2">
            <span class="px-1.5 py-0.5 border border-neutral-300 dark:border-neutral-700 bg-white dark:bg-neutral-900 text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300 text-[11px] font-mono font-bold rounded">Q2</span>
            <h3 class="font-bold text-neutral-900 dark:text-white text-base">Transfer Impact & Gap Closure</h3>
          </div>
          <p class="text-sm text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
            "Do new signings and tactical changes offer a surgical remedy to the weaknesses exposed in Question 1?"
          </p>
          <div class="text-xs text-neutral-500 dark:text-neutral-400 pt-1 leading-relaxed">
            <strong>Evaluation:</strong> Analyzing incoming vs outgoing players through the lens of team needs. Does recruitment solve structural bottlenecks or duplicate depth in redundant areas?
          </div>
        </div>

        <!-- Question 3 -->
        <div class="p-4 rounded border border-neutral-200 dark:border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-50 dark:bg-[#18181b] space-y-1.5">
          <div class="flex items-center gap-2">
            <span class="px-1.5 py-0.5 border border-neutral-300 dark:border-neutral-700 bg-white dark:bg-neutral-900 text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300 text-[11px] font-mono font-bold rounded">Q3</span>
            <h3 class="font-bold text-neutral-900 dark:text-white text-base">The Bottom Line: Performance Models & Projections</h3>
          </div>
          <p class="text-sm text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
            "Based on historical baseline performance plus transfer delta – what is the realistic points ceiling and projected league finish?"
          </p>
          <div class="text-xs text-neutral-500 dark:text-neutral-400 pt-1 leading-relaxed space-y-1">
            <p>
              <strong>Evaluation:</strong> Combining the baseline squad rating with the "upgrade delta" (goals prevented + goals and chances added) to estimate Expected Points (xPTS) and final table projections.
            </p>
            <p>
              <strong>Delta Formula:</strong> <code class="font-mono text-[11px] text-neutral-900 dark:text-white bg-white dark:bg-neutral-900 px-1 py-0.5 rounded border border-neutral-200 dark:border-neutral-700">Net Delta = Total Contribution (In) - Total Loss (Out)</code>
            </p>
          </div>
        </div>
