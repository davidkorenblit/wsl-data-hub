    </p>

    <p>

    <p class="font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-white">
      That metric is Expected Points (xPTS) versus actual points collected on the pitch.
    </p>

    <p>
      On the surface, there is no straightforward statistical explanation for why Arsenal consistently drops just enough points to surrender the crown.
    </p>

    <div class="p-5 rounded bg-neutral-50 dark:bg-[#18181b] border border-neutral-200 dark:border-neutral-800 space-y-3 mt-4">
      <div class="text-xs font-mono uppercase text-neutral-900 dark:text-white font-bold tracking-wider">
        The Analytical Vector
      </div>
      <p class="text-base text-neutral-900 dark:text-white font-medium">
        There is one potential hypothesis—or at least one decisive inquiry worth isolating, because it is the only avenue that accounts for this recurring anomaly.
      </p>
      <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
        And that hypothesis is: <strong>scrutinizing how Arsenal starts seasons compared to how they finish them.</strong><br>
        Maybe that is where the crack lies, maybe not—and maybe this season, with their newly reinforced depth, the past will finally cease to dictate the future.
      </p>
