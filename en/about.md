      Hey! If you landed here, you're either a friend of mine whom I dragged here against your will, or you genuinely care about the FA WSL and women's football at large.
    </p>

    <p class="text-xl font-bold text-neutral-900 dark:text-white">
      Either way — welcome. It's truly great to have you here.
    </p>

    <p>
      As anyone following the women's game knows, there is a massive hunger for detailed, data-informed storytelling, tactical breakdowns, and honest coverage of the sport we love. I decided it was time to build a dedicated hub for exactly that.
    </p>

    <p>
      By trade, I'm a computer scientist and data professional. Consequently, much of what you'll find here is rooted in underlying metrics, expected models, and tactical patterns. Some pieces will be purely numbers-driven. Others will be passionately, unapologetically tied to the club I support — <strong>Tottenham Hotspur</strong> — which you'll easily identify because the voice will be much more that of a dedicated fan than a neutral analyst.
    </p>

    <p>
      Thank you for reading! If you like what you find, share it with fellow fans. The game is growing at a thrilling pace, and it deserves deep, dedicated analysis for the best league in the world (yes, that's my deliberate hook to start the WSL vs. NWSL debate).
    </p>
