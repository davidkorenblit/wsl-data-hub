# WSL Season Preview & Performance Analysis

This analysis provides a comprehensive overview of the Women's Super League (WSL), focusing on team dynamics and the shifting competitive landscape. By leveraging historical data and advanced metrics, we aim to project league competitiveness for the upcoming season and evaluate how it impacts performance and player outcomes.

## League Context

The WSL season kicks off on September 4th. Traditionally, the league has been defined by a "Big Three"—Arsenal, Chelsea, and Manchester City—who have dominated league titles and historical performance. However, the emergence of London City Lionesses (LCL) under Michelle Kang's ownership is disrupting this hierarchy. Following a sixth-place finish in their debut season, LCL has aggressively recruited world-class talent, including Alexia Putellas, Mapi León, and Mary Earps. This project explores whether LCL is now a genuine contender for the title.

## Key Analytical Questions

* **Baseline Performance:** Where did LCL rank in key statistical categories during their debut season?  
* **Strengths and Weaknesses:** What were the key strengths and weaknesses of the previous season?  
* **Acquisition Impact:** How well do the new signings fit the team's needs, and do they address systemic weaknesses?  
* **Structural Shifts:** How significantly does the new roster change the team's overall tactical identity?  
* **Macro Variables:** Does the lack of European football provide a competitive edge over the Top 3 through lower fixture congestion?

# Methodology: The Roadmap to Answers

## Phase 1: Data Ingestion

* **Team-Level Metrics:** Gathering standard stats, shooting, passing, and defensive data for LCL and the wider WSL field using FBref.  
* **Player Profiles:** Analyzing historical performance from Liga F and the UWCL to profile new signings.  
* **Advanced Goalkeeping:** Using PSxG+/- to isolate the specific impact of world-class goalkeeping.

## Phase 2: Data Processing & Normalization

* **Filtering & Normalization:** Standardizing metrics "Per 90" and filtering for significant playing time to reduce noise.  
* **League Adjustments:** Applying multipliers to Liga F data to align expectations with WSL intensity levels.  
* **Replacement Value:** Comparing incoming talent against outgoing players to calculate the net squad "Delta."

## Phase 3: Insights & Outlook

* **Visual Evidence:** Using Team Scatter Plots and Player Radar Charts to visualize our conclusions.

**Predictive Forecast:** Projecting xPTS improvement to determine if LCL is ready for a top-tier European push this season.
